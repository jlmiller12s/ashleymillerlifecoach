The 8-Week Journey

Your Next AssignMEANT Roadmap

Eight focused weeks organized into our four core coaching phases.

Phase 1

Orientation

Weeks 1 & 2
  • Week 1: Arrive — Lay down the exhaustion. Name where you actually are today without judgment.
  • Week 2: Reflect — Audit your time, energy leaks, and the stories you've been telling yourself.
Phase 2

Clean Slate

Weeks 3 & 4
  • Week 3: Recognize — Identify people-pleasing, guilt, and where you've been waiting for a permission slip.
  • Week 4: Release — Loving closure on obligations that don't belong in this new chapter.
Phase 3

Lesson Plan

Weeks 5 & 6
  • Week 5: Envision — Pull the God-given dream out of the shadows. What does winning look like?
  • Week 6: Strategize — Map the exact milestones, boundaries, and calendar architecture.
Phase 4

Field Trip

Weeks 7 & 8
  • Week 7: Respond — Take the first real, visible public step while having Ashley in your ear.
  • Week 8: Move Forward — Finalize your long-term action plan and celebrate the breakthrough.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to be a devout Christian to work with you?

Not at all. My faith in Jesus deeply informs my worldview, character, and values, and I regularly pray for my clients. However, I never force religion on anyone. Wherever you are spiritually, you will find a safe, welcoming, and empowering environment.

What if I don't know what my dream or goal even is yet?

That is completely normal! That is exactly why Phase 1 is Orientation and Phase 2 is Clean Slate. Most women who come to me just know they are restless, exhausted, or in transition. We discover the vision together.

How is this different from Homeroom?

Homeroom is our year-long group coaching classroom with weekly live calls and a vibrant member community. 1:1 Coaching is a high-touch, customized 8-week sprint dedicated 100% to your specific life, calendar, and goals.
