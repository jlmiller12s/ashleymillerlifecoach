My Story

Former teacher. Forever student. Your next chapter's biggest fan.

For 16+ years, my world was classrooms, lesson plans, rehearsals, and pouring everything into students and teams.

I loved it. But deep down, I knew what it felt like to be exhausted, holding up everyone else's world while my own God-given dreams stayed parked in the "someday when I have time" category.

I kept waiting for the perfect moment. Waiting for someone with authority to tap me on the shoulder and say, "You're qualified now. Go do it."

Spoiler alert: nobody handed me a permission slip. And one day, sitting with the Lord, I realized I didn't need one. He had already called me. Walking away from the classroom wasn't walking away from teaching—it was expanding the classroom to women like you who are ready to rise.

Core Convictions

What I Believe

The foundation underneath every call, worksheet, and coaching conversation.

01

You don't need a permission slip

The vision and gifts God placed in your heart are proof enough. You don't need anyone's signature to take your calling seriously.

02

Clarity comes from movement

You cannot steer a parked car. Overthinking won't unlock your next chapter; taking one small, obedient step will.

03

Faith is an invitation, not a lecture

We invite Jesus into the messy middle of your schedule, fears, and doubts. No preachy religious guilt—just honest discipleship.

04

You can love your family and your purpose

Being faithful to your home doesn't mean forsaking the gifts God put in you. Wholehearted moms raise wholehearted kids.
