Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Thank you for investing in yourself with Ashley Miller Life Coach. We honor your commitment, your time, and the sacred opportunity to walk alongside you in your next chapter.

Digital Downloads & Workbooks (e.g. The Peace Promise)

Due to the immediate digital delivery and non-returnable nature of downloadable workbooks and audio files, all digital product sales are final and non-refundable.

Homeroom Group Coaching

Homeroom membership is billed on an annual auto-renew basis. You may cancel your renewal at any time prior to your next renewal date inside your Skool account settings. Due to instant access to our coaching vault and member forum, past subscription payments are non-refundable.

1:1 Coaching Engagements

Because private 1:1 coaching reserves dedicated calendar spots, custom preparation, and limited client capacity, 1:1 coaching payments and deposits are non-refundable. Rescheduling policy: Clients may reschedule individual coaching sessions with at least 24 hours advance notice.

Rooted Women In-Person Experiences & Retreats

Ticket sales and retreat deposits for in-person events cover venue, catering, and materials committed in advance. Refunds for ticket cancellations are permitted up to 14 days prior to the event date. Tickets are fully transferable to another woman with notice to our team.
