Our Origin

How Rooted Women Began

It started at Increase: A Night of Vision. We brought together women from all walks of life—moms, professionals, creatives, and dreamers—who were carrying heavy loads, running on fumes, and yearning for spiritual depth.

In a world constantly telling women to hustle harder, compete, and figure it out on their own, God reminded us of Colossians 2:7: "Rooted and built up in Him, strengthened in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thankfulness."

Rooted Women Collective was created as an oasis: Jesus at the center, community at the heart, and room for you to simply breathe.
