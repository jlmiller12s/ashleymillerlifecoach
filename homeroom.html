Got Questions?

Homeroom FAQ

Can I join after November 30?

Yes, you can join anytime! However, the special $47 Founder Price is strictly locked in through November 30, 2026. On December 1, enrollment increases to the standard $97/year.

What if I can't attend the weekly live calls?

Every single live call is recorded and placed inside your Skool member vault. You can watch or listen anytime on your phone or laptop, plus submit questions in advance for Ashley to answer on the call!

Is this the same as 1:1 coaching?

No. Homeroom is a community and group coaching space where Ashley teaches and coaches members live on calls. If you desire private, customized one-on-one mentorship tailored exclusively to your personal roadmap, check out 1:1 Coaching.
